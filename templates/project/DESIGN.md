## Authority

For user-visible work, this document owns durable visual-system decisions after
`PRODUCT.md` and before a surface brief in `docs/surfaces/`. `PRODUCT.md` owns
product truth and constraints; a surface brief records one approved composition
and interaction direction. A lower authority must not silently override a
higher one.
